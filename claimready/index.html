Your home inventory, ready for any claim

ClaimReady helps you photograph and list what you own, room by room, so that after a fire, flood or burglary you can show your insurer exactly what was lost.

Rooms and itemsPhotos, value, purchase date, brand, model, serial number and warranty for every item.
Receipts and labelsKeep receipts as photos or PDFs. On-device text recognition fills in serial numbers, prices and dates.
Totals and coverageSee what your home is worth by room and category, compared with your policy's coverage limit.
Private by designEverything stays on your phone. No account, no tracking, no ads.
